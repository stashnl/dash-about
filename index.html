Dash

Dash is a personal study planner. It is used by one person, its owner, to see their own course deadlines, readings and tasks in one place. It is not offered to anyone else and has no sign-up.

Privacy policy

  • Dash asks Google for access to the owner's own Google Tasks and Google Calendar, only so that the owner's deadlines and tasks appear in their planner and stay in step with Google.
  • The data stays on a computer controlled by the owner. It is not sold, shared, given to third parties or used for advertising, and it is not used to train any AI model.
  • The owner can withdraw access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Dash's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Terms

Dash is a private tool for its owner's own use, provided as is.
